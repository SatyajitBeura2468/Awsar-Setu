# Product Spec

AwsarSetu helps citizens across India discover, understand, save, track and act on opportunities.

## Promise

Find opportunities made for your next step.

Supporting text: Scholarships, jobs, vacancies, schemes, training and support, all in one clear place.

## Audience

Every citizen of India from day one. English is default and Hindi is a complete alternate language. Future regional languages should reuse the same translation structure.

## Browse-First Model

Visitors can browse Home, Explore, Vacancies, opportunity details, guides and official-source links without an account.

Accounts add:

- Cross-device saved opportunities
- Application tracking
- Personalised matches
- Browser/app notifications
- Rare meaningful email alerts
- Lightweight profile preferences

## Required Main Routes

- `/`
- `/explore`
- `/vacancies`
- `/saved`
- `/account`
- `/opportunities/[slug]`
- `/guides/[slug]`
- `/privacy`
- `/terms`
- `/offline`

## Matching Language

AwsarSetu can say:

- Likely Match
- Possible Match
- Check Criteria

It must never say guaranteed eligible.
