# AwsarSetu V5 Design System

## North star

AwsarSetu V5 is an Opportunity Navigation System: editorial enough to feel considered, structured enough to feel trustworthy, and alive enough to make the next step feel possible.

## Visual tokens

- Canvas: cool paper `#f4f8fc`
- Surface: true white `#ffffff`
- Ink: midnight `#0b1f3a`
- Muted: slate `#5c6b80`
- Civic blue: `#0b5bd3`
- Trust teal: `#078473`
- Direction saffron: `#ef8b2c`
- Hairline: `#dbe5f0`
- Display type: Georgia/Cambria editorial serif
- UI type: Noto Sans Variable and Noto Sans Devanagari Variable

## Container model

Prefer open bands, rails, timelines and lists. Cards are reserved for dialogs, focused settings surfaces and selected-detail panes. Major sections use hairline rules, not giant rounded wrappers.

## Signature motifs

- Route line: progress, connection and navigation.
- Verified node: official-source trust.
- Deadline pulse: time-sensitive action only when a verified deadline exists.
- Journey rail: saved-to-applied progress.

## Motion

- Route paths reveal on initial entry.
- Opportunity nodes pulse once, then remain quiet.
- Rows translate by at most two pixels on hover.
- Sheets use short opacity/translation transitions.
- Reduced-motion mode removes path drawing and spatial transitions without hiding content.

## Guardrails

- No fake opportunity counts or invented notices.
- No government affiliation or endorsement claim.
- No guaranteed eligibility language.
- No Aadhaar, bank details, exact birth date or sensitive-document collection.
- All official-source actions retain the source preflight.
